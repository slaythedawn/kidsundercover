Kids Under Cover provide one and two bedroom studios to families at risk of becoming homeless. The studio program is coupled with the education scholarship program, ensuring young people have access to much needed educational resources. Kids Under Cover strengthens young people’s connection to family, community and education, and make a lasting difference in their lives.

## Your support is very much appreciated.

Thank you for your contribution to Kids Under Cover for the inaugural Atora Games. You can pick from an amount and see instantly the impact you’ll have, or choose your own value and complete the form. Donations of $2 or more are tax deductible.

Without the support of like-minded people, we could not continue to pursue our vision of a community where no young person is homeless.

Funds raised are used to support Kids Under Cover’s work to prevent homelessness among young people by providing the foundations to strengthen their connection to family, community and education.

## Your donation will help us to end youth homelessness

Kids Under Cover has helped over 4,000 young people and awarded over 2,200 scholarships. Right now we have more than 600 studios on the ground, housing 900-plus young people, helping them stay connected with their families, community and education.

## About Kids Under Cover

Who benefits from your car donation? Young people aged between 12-25 years old who are at risk of becoming homeless; or who in some cases, have already experienced homelessness.

Kids Under Cover has been around since 1989, when the organisation was founded by well-known business person and philanthropist, Ken Morgan AO. Ken started as a car sales person before dedicating his life’s work to ending youth homelessness. As the owner of multiple car dealerships around Melbourne, he was perfectly placed to set up the Donate Your Car initiative to fund Kids Under Cover’s incredible work.
