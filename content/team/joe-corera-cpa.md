Joe came on board the Kids Under Cover team in March 2023 as the Chief Financial Officer.

He began his career in banking and finance and has built his career in accounting, insurance, logistics and health. He has held senior positions in the corporate, government and not-for-profit sectors and brings a strategic return on investment approach, balanced with a community focus.

Joe is passionate about using resources in a way that maximises benefits to the community.
