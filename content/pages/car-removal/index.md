      - question: Will you remove a car that doesn't run?
        answer: Yes, as long as the battery and engine are both intact and nothing has been removed from the vehicle.
      - question: Will you take a scrap car?
        answer: We can take old cars with general wear and tear, but not anything with significant damage, such as smashed windows, major panel damage, mould or missing parts. For a badly damaged car, a wrecker is likely the better option.
      - question: Does car removal cost anything?
        answer: Within metropolitan Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Adelaide, Perth, Darwin and Hobart, you can make an optional contribution towards towing. In regional areas towing costs are higher, so we'll ask if you're able to contribute towards them. If your car is driveable, you can drop it off yourself and receive a $100 eGift Card.
      - question: How much will my car sell for?
        answer: All donated vehicles are sold by Manheim's experienced auctioneers at their public auctions. The car is worth exactly what someone is prepared to pay for it at auction, which might be more than, equal to or less than you expect.
      - question: Will I get a tax receipt?
        answer: Yes. All vehicles donated to Kids Under Cover are tax deductible. You'll receive your tax receipt for the sale amount within 8 to 12 weeks, issued in the name of the last or current registered owner.
