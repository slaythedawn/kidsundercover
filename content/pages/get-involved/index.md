The success of the work we do depends on collaboration with supporters, partners and the broader community. Every contribution takes us one step closer to our vision of a community where every young person has a stable place to live and from which to build a brighter future.

There are countless ways to support the work we do to end youth homelessness. Here are just a few:

## Make a donation

Every little bit makes a huge difference to the work we do. Your support enables us to deliver our programs and reach more families and young people in need.

[Make a donation](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/donate/)

## Donate your car

The Donate Your Car program is one of our most important fundraising streams. It’s free, tax deductible and an easy alternative to selling or trading your unwanted car, motorbike, boat or caravan! We'll pick up your vehicle, auction it off and all proceeds go directly towards funding our programs.

[Donate your car](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/donate-your-car/)

## Bequests

Leaving a gift in your Will is one of the most generous legacies you can leave. Your gift will help enable us to continue developing innovative solutions to the challenges young people face, now and into the future.

[Bequests](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/bequests/)

## Community fundraising

Fundraising is at the heart of what we do. It’s a community effort, and you can be a crucial part of it.

[Community fundraising](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/community-fundraising/)

## Corporate and business partnerships

We are grateful for the unwavering support from our corporate partners, and we invite businesses like yours to join us in making a lasting impact on the lives of young people.

[Corporate and business partnerships](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/corporate-business-partnerships/)

## Work with us

Working with Kids Under Cover is rewarding and challenging. Working with us provides a wonderful opportunity to make a real difference in the lives of young people.

[Work with us](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/work-with-us/)
