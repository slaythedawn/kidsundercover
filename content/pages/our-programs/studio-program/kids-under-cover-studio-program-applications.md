    text: We provide **one and two-bedroom, fully relocatable studios** (with bathroom) constructed on the grounds of the family or carer's home.
    body: |
      The Studio Program is at the core of Kids Under Cover work in preventing youth homelessness.

      The studio keeps young people connected to their family or carer and reduces the risk of them being forced to leave home prematurely.

  - type: split
    id: eligibility
    nav: Eligibility
    title: Eligibility criteria
    body: |
      Each studio provides a stable, secure and private space for a young person experiencing challenges that can lead to youth homelessness, such as household overcrowding, domestic violence, substance abuse, mental health concerns and contact with the youth justice system.

      To be eligible for a Kids Under Cover studio a young person must be:
    points:
      - Aged between **12 and 25 years**
      - Already supported by a **registered Community Service Organisation**
      - Identified as having **demonstrated risk of homelessness**
    image: https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/studio-program-montage-7.jpg

  - type: split
    id: costs
    nav: Costs
    tone: card
    title: Costs
    body: |
      **There is no cost to the applicant or family.** Kids Under Cover fund the build and installation of the studio through generous donations from individuals, trusts & foundations, and government support.

      Kids Under Cover audit studios and pay for maintenance as a result of normal wear and tear. Any maintenance that is a result of wilful damage by the occupant or other individuals must be paid for by the family.
    image: https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/studio-program-montage-8.jpg

    yes:
      title: To be suitable for a studio
      items:
        - The property on which the studio is to be placed must have a backyard large enough for a studio
        - The studio must be intended for use by a young person/s aged between 12 and 25 years
    note: A simple yet rigorous application process ensures that studios are allocated to young people in greatest need.

  - type: steps
    style: numbered
    tone: card
    title: How an application works
    items:
      - icon: people
        title: Referral
        text: "Our Community Service Organisation (CSO) partners work directly with the young person to support their social needs. Through this work, the social worker decides if a Kids Under Cover studio, combined with the existing support, is the best way to address the young person's risk of homelessness. The social worker then applies to Kids Under Cover on behalf of the young person and their family."
      - icon: studio
        title: Assessment and allocation
        text: "The Kids Under Cover team assesses the application and lets the social worker know the outcome. If the application is approved and funding has been allocated, we work with builders, local councils, the social worker and the family to plan the studio installation. After completion, Kids Under Cover looks after ongoing maintenance. When the family no longer needs the studio, it is dismantled and moved to another family in need."
