    yes:
      title: When choosing to support an organisation like Kids Under Cover to do great work in the community, please remember
      items:
        - Making donations is voluntary and always an individual donor’s choice. You might decide on your own to make a donation, or you might be asked, by letter, email, phone or in person, to donate.
        - Before you commit to a donation, check to make sure you are contributing via a legitimate donation channel for that charity.
        - Check for the charity’s name on the ACNC Charity Register.
        - Ask for identification from anyone seeking a donation from you in a face-to-face or telephone setting.
        - Be careful of online requests for donations, by checking to make sure that the webpage or email is genuine.
