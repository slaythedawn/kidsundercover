      - text: Most countries that measure crowding in relation to homelessness utilise a statistical or space-based measure.
      - text: Space-based measures fail to account for the impact of the social dimension on members of households feeling crowded.
      - text: The literature addressing crowding often fails to conceptualise crowding within the social and the spatial.
      - text: On integrating the social and the spatial within the research on risks that homelessness crowding poses for young people, **a more complex picture emerges of the impact of crowding**.
