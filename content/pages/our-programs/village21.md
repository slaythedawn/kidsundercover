Our pilot Village 21 was developed in 2021 by Kids Under Cover with Anglicare Victoria to provide a safe and secure shared living environment for young people aged 18-21. Beyond offering a place to live, Village 21 empowers young people with crucial employment guidance, educational pathways, and life skills for a successful transition to independence.

The Village 21 pilot, in Melbourne’s North, has been active since 2021 and in that time has led to positive outcomes for the young people living there. Since then, another two new Village 21s have launched in partnership with Aboriginal Community-Controlled Organisations (ACCOs) including Victorian Aboriginal Child Care Agency (VACCA) and Gippsland and East Gippsland Aboriginal Cooperative Ltd (GEGAC).

The model provides stable, self-sufficient accommodation for up to six young people aged 18 to 21 as well as two live-in mentors. It also employs a Key Practitioner who helps the young people access services, social activities and employment, education and training pathways. Each studio within the village offers residents their own private living space while a communal area encourages shared cooking, conversation and social interaction.

## Village 21 Preston

The Village 21 pilot has now been active since 2021 with many successes in delivering positive outcomes for young people living in this fit-for-purpose accommodation.

Village 21 empowers young people exiting out-of-home care with crucial employment guidance, educational pathways, and life skills for a successful transition to independence via the support provided by our partners at Anglicare Victoria. The Village 21 is designed to be a safe and secure shared living environment to enable effective delivery of those wrap around supports.

Our partnership with Anglicare Victoria serves as a blueprint as we lay the foundation for additional Village 21 sites across the state and beyond.

[About Village 21 Preston](https://www.kuc.org.au/our-programs/village21preston/)

## Moorumbina Mongurnallin

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/1-1024x1024.png)

In consultation with the Victorian Aboriginal Child Care Agency (VACCA), Anglicare Victoria and with support from DFFH, Kids Under Cover has created ‘Moorumbina Mongurnallin’, a Village 21 housing project to be delivered in Melbourne’s South East, to accommodate young First Nations people aged 18-21 who are leaving care. VACCA employs the Key Practitioner and live-in mentors, who support the young people during their tenure in the Village. Moorumbina Mongurnallin translates to ‘You are Loved’ in Bunurong language, a name suggested by one of the Village’s prospective inaugural occupants. The site has been designed with on-site amenities and cultural adaptations identified via consultation with Elders and the community. It accommodates six young residents for up to three years.

[About Moorumbina Mongurnallin](https://www.kuc.org.au/our-programs/moorumbina-mongurnallin/)

## Upcoming Village 21 projects

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2-1024x1024.png)

The Bairnsdale Village 21 project has now been completed and is entering the onboarding phase for young people, with a particular focus on supporting First Nations young people. The project has been designed with a strong emphasis on cultural safety, inclusion and community connection.

The Shepparton Village 21 project will soon be under construction and is expected to commence onboarding young people in July/August 2026, further expanding Kids Under Cover’s capacity to provide integrated housing and support responses for vulnerable young people in regional Victoria.

[About Village 21 Bairnsdale](https://www.kuc.org.au/our-programs/village21bairnsdale/)
