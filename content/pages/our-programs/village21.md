    text: Our pilot Village 21 was developed in 2021 by Kids Under Cover with Anglicare Victoria to provide a **safe and secure shared living environment for young people aged 18 to 21**.
    body: |
      Beyond offering a place to live, Village 21 empowers young people with crucial employment guidance, educational pathways, and life skills for a successful transition to independence.

      The Village 21 pilot, in Melbourne's North, has been active since 2021 and in that time has led to positive outcomes for the young people living there. Since then, another two new Village 21s have launched in partnership with Aboriginal Community-Controlled Organisations (ACCOs) including Victorian Aboriginal Child Care Agency (VACCA) and Gippsland and East Gippsland Aboriginal Cooperative Ltd (GEGAC).

  - type: split
    id: moorumbina-mongurnallin
    nav: Moorumbina Mongurnallin
    tone: card
    eyebrow: Melbourne's South East
    title: Moorumbina Mongurnallin
    body: |
      In consultation with the Victorian Aboriginal Child Care Agency (VACCA), Anglicare Victoria and with support from DFFH, Kids Under Cover has created 'Moorumbina Mongurnallin', a Village 21 housing project to be delivered in Melbourne's South East, to accommodate young First Nations people aged 18 to 21 who are leaving care. VACCA employs the Key Practitioner and live-in mentors, who support the young people during their tenure in the Village.

      Moorumbina Mongurnallin translates to 'You are Loved' in Bunurong language, a name suggested by one of the Village's prospective inaugural occupants. The site has been designed with on-site amenities and cultural adaptations identified via consultation with Elders and the community. It accommodates six young residents for up to three years.
