  - type: split
    id: the-studio
    nav: The studio
    tone: card
    eyebrow: Studio design
    title: Built to last, and to keep families close
    body: |
      Our studios are sustainable, heat efficient and durable, constructed from Durra Panel, a sustainably sourced building material produced in Bendigo that insulates against sound and heat while being stronger, safer, and superior to plasterboard. Durra Panel is made from reclaimed wheat straw waste that is 100% recyclable and biodegradable.
    points:
      - Each studio includes a **bathroom**
      - A **study nook in each bedroom**, so the young person can have a suitable place to study and relax
      - '**No kitchen**, an intentional design choice that encourages young people to stay connected to their families and carers at key moments like meal times'

      - icon: tools
        title: Made in Bendigo
        text: Studios are prefabricated by our construction partners at Ortech in Bendigo, packed up as a flat-packed kit with pre-assembled frames.
      - icon: truck
        title: Delivered by truck
        text: The kit is transported via truck to the property where it will be installed.
      - icon: studio
        title: Built in the backyard
        text: Our builders at Harris HMC assemble the kit in-situ in the family's backyard. Erected and fully fitted within 10 days.

        text: Once the studio is no longer required, it can be relocated. Every studio is demountable and can be flat-packed for relocation up to four times, then craned onto a truck to provide relief to more young people and their families.
