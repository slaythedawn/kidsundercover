    title: Before you donate
    items:
      - question: What kinds of vehicles will you accept?
        answer: |
          We accept cars, vans, motorbikes, caravans, trucks, buses, tractors, boats on trailers, and much more. Have a different vehicle? Contact us to find out if we can accept your donation.

          We are able to accept vehicles that have general wear and tear, however we're unable to accept anything with significant damage, such as smashed windows, major panel damage, mould or missing parts.

          We're able to accept cars that aren't running, as long as the battery and engine are both intact and nothing has been removed from the vehicle.
      - question: Why should I donate my car to Kids Under Cover?
        answer: |
          100% of the proceeds from the sale of your vehicle go to Kids Under Cover, a not for profit organisation that prevents youth homelessness.

          It's a quick, easy and free way to get rid of an unwanted vehicle, and your vehicle donation is 100% tax deductible.
      - question: Can I donate my car if there is a recall for the model I own?
        answer: Please make sure your car does not have an active recall before you decide to donate it and fill out the donation form. Contact your vehicle's manufacturer for details on how to fix an active recall.
      - question: My vehicle has finance owing. Can I donate it?
        answer: |
          We cannot accept donated vehicles that still have finance owing. Please make sure your contract has been terminated before donating the vehicle.

          If a donated vehicle is found to have finance owing, we will contact you to resolve it.
      - question: Does my car have to be registered?
        answer: No. We sell all cars unregistered at auction to avoid any transfer of ownership or notice of disposal paperwork. If your car is registered, we will ask you to contact your local road authority and cancel the registration. If you're still driving the car, you can cancel the registration 1 to 2 business days after the car has been collected, or before collection if that's easier for you.
      - question: Can I donate an abandoned car?
        answer: No. Legally we are not able to accept abandoned vehicles. If there is an abandoned car in your street, please call your local council.
      - question: What's the environmental impact of donating my car?
        answer: The Donate Your Car program is based on social reinvestment, promoting the reuse of vehicles. It extends the vehicle's life, reduces landfill and supports a circular economy. It's an ethical and environmentally aligned way of funding our work preventing youth homelessness.

      - question: How do I fill out the Donate Your Car form?
        answer: |
          - Have the contact details of the person organising collection of the vehicle ready, as well as the location of the car.
          - Have the details of the current or previous registration ready, including the VIN. It's a 17 character mix of letters and numbers with no I, Q or O (I is 1 and O is 0).
          - Check whether the car starts, and have photos of any extensive damage on your device so you can upload them with the form.

          If you're having trouble with the online form, you can complete the [Word version of the form](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Donate-Your-Car-donation-form.docx) and email it to [donateyourcar@kuc.org.au](mailto:donateyourcar@kuc.org.au).

          All vehicles are sold at public auction, and any transfer of ownership is done with the advice of your transport authority. Kids Under Cover does not take ownership of donated vehicles and is not the new owner. Please read our [terms and conditions](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/DYC-Ts-and-Cs-2016_Websiteversion2016.pdf).

          Need more help with the form? Call us on **1800 801 633** or email [donateyourcar@kuc.org.au](mailto:donateyourcar@kuc.org.au).

      - question: Where will you collect vehicles from?
        answer: |
          We collect vehicles within metropolitan Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Adelaide, Perth, Darwin and Hobart.

          We can collect cars from regional areas, however as towing costs are higher we will ask if you are able to [contribute towards the cost of towing](/donate-your-car-towing-contributions/). If the car is driveable, you can also drop it off at the closest Manheim auction site. See our [drop off locations](/get-involved/donate-your-car-drop-off-locations/).
      - question: How long will it take for my vehicle to be picked up?
        answer: |
          Once we receive your donation form, we will contact you within 3 business days to organise collection. We then need to give our transport company at least 5 business days' notice. All up, it takes about one to two weeks.

          If you need an urgent collection (because of a council notice, or because you are moving overseas, for example), please contact us after completing the form so we can organise an earlier date.
      - question: Do I need to be there while you collect my car?
        answer: |
          No, you do not need to be there. There's no need to stay home that day or take time off work.

          You can sort out details such as keys directly with the towing company when they call to confirm the collection, and let them know how to get to the vehicle. You can also nominate someone else, such as a family member, as the point of contact.
      - question: Who will pick up my donated vehicle?
        answer: |
          All transport is organised through our auction partner Manheim. They use fully licensed and insured towing companies to pick up cars across the country.

          You will get a courtesy call before collection, so you know who to expect. This may be the day before, usually late afternoon or early evening, or at the latest by 10am on the morning of collection. Please contact us if you haven't heard from the transport company by 10am on your collection day so we can follow up.

  - type: faq
    id: tax
    nav: Sale and tax
    tone: card
    title: Auction and your tax receipt
    items:
      - question: How much money will my car sell for at auction?
        answer: All our donated vehicles are sold by Manheim's experienced auctioneers at their public auctions. Regardless of what you believe your car might be worth, it is worth exactly what someone is prepared to pay for it at auction. This might be more than, equal to or less than you expect.
      - question: Will I receive a tax receipt for my car donation?
        answer: |
          Yes, all vehicles donated to Kids Under Cover are tax deductible. You can expect your tax receipt for the auction sale amount within 8 to 12 weeks of submitting your donation. It will be sent by Australia Post unless you ask otherwise.

          The date on your receipt will be the date Kids Under Cover received the sale proceeds. The receipt must be issued in the name of the last or current registered owner of the vehicle.
      - question: What is the ATO valuation process?
        answer: |
          If you think your vehicle has a current market value of over $5,000, you are eligible for a valuation by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO). Your tax deductible receipt will then be based on the ATO valuation amount.

          If the ATO values your vehicle at less than $5,000, you are not entitled to a tax deduction based on their valuation and they will not issue a Certificate of Valuation.

          You can't get an ATO valuation before deciding to donate. The ATO only values property that has already been donated. For more information, call the ATO's Not-for-profit department on 1300 130 428 or read about [tax deductible gifts on the ATO website](https://www.ato.gov.au/Individuals/Income-and-deductions/Deductions-you-can-claim/Other-deductions/Gifts-and-donations/).
      - question: How does the ATO valuation work, step by step?
        answer: |
          1. Complete our donation form.
          2. Our team will contact you to discuss eligibility and send you the paperwork to complete, with details about paying the ATO application fee of $241 (also tax deductible).
          3. Complete the forms and post them to us.
          4. We arrange collection of your vehicle, which is stored at Manheim until the ATO values it. The ATO aims to complete most valuations within a month.
          5. Once we receive your forms, we sign them and send them to the ATO for you.
          6. The ATO values your vehicle and sends you a Certificate of Valuation.
          7. Email us a copy of your certificate and we will issue your tax deductible receipt based on the ATO valuation amount.
