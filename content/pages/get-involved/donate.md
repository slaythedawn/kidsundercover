    body: |
      As a foster carer, Rachel has welcomed more than 100 vulnerable kids into her home. So when her biological children began to grow, and space became scarce, it broke her heart to begin turning children away.

      The two-bedroom Kids Under Cover studio changed everything for Rachel's family. Her sons Jacob and Finn had the privacy and quiet they needed, and it freed up bedroom space in the main house so Rachel could foster more vulnerable children.

      > "I can't foster without the studio because my biological children need a room. Without it, we wouldn't have the space to welcome others… and I wouldn't have been able to help anywhere near the number I have."
