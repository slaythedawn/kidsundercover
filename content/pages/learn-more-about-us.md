We install relocatable, one or two-bedroom studios in the backyard of a family or carer’s home. Our [Studio Program](https://www.kuc.org.au/our-programs/studio-program/) helps relieve overcrowding, eases tension and provides young people with a safe and stable home environment.

We’re proud of our strong history working alongside Aboriginal communities, families, and young people, in partnership with leading Aboriginal organisations and community leaders.
