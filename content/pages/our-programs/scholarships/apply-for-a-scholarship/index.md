It also gives them the freedom to choose subjects and pathways based on passion and potential, not just affordability, keeping them engaged, motivated and future-focused.

  - type: checklist
    id: eligibility
    nav: Eligibility
    title: Am I eligible?
    intro: You can apply to receive a Kids Under Cover Scholarship if you
    yes:
      title: You can apply if you
      items:
        - Are aged **12 to 25 years** as of 30 June 2026
        - Are undertaking secondary schooling, TAFE, university and job training
        - Live on the same premises as a **Kids Under Cover studio**
        - Household has completed the acquittal process for previously awarded scholarship/s
    note: Previous scholarship recipients are still eligible to receive a scholarship in 2026.

    title: What can the money be used for?
    intro: You just need to keep all receipts for purchases with your scholarship funds.

    no:
      title: Costs not covered

        - Non-Government School Fees
        - Fees incurred by someone other than the scholarship recipient
    note: If you are unsure about a particular item, visit our [spending guide](/our-programs/scholarships/apply-for-a-scholarship/scholarship-program-spending-guide/), or contact us.

  - type: faq
    id: faqs
    nav: FAQs
    title: Scholarship FAQs
    intro: Find out more information about the Kids Under Cover Scholarship application process and how to submit the documentation required for expenses funded through the program.
    items:
      - question: Do I need to keep receipts?
        answer: Yes, all receipts and invoices used for scholarship funds must be kept and emailed to [scholarship@kuc.org.au](mailto:scholarship@kuc.org.au).
      - question: How much will I receive?
        answer: |
          Successful applicants will receive a set amount, depending upon the level of study or training they are undertaking in 2025.

          Apprentices will be awarded a scholarship based on their age during the application.

          Please note: you will need to specify planned expenses at the time of application.
      - question: What time period is covered by the scholarship?
        answer: The 2026 scholarships can only be used to cover educational expenses incurred in 2026.
