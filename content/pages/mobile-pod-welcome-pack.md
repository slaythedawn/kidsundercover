Kids Under Cover is a not-for-profit organisation that works to prevent youth homelessness. Our Mobile Pod Program provides extra space to ease the pressure of overcrowding, support young people to stay connected to family or carers and provide a safe, secure, and stable environment to help young people thrive.

> **If anything breaks down or needs repairing, please call your referring caseworker as soon as possible. The sooner they are advised, the better, so the problem can be fixed straight away and minimise any lasting damage.**

Below is information that will help you in maintenance of the mobile pod.

Once again, welcome to your Kids Under Cover Mobile Pod and we hope you will be very happy in your new home.
