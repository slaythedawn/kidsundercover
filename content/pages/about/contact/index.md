To help us respond to you as quickly as we can, please leave us a short description of how we can help. Don’t forget to select the type of enquiry you have for us so the most appropriate team can receive your message.

**In the event of an emergency, please call 000 to reach police, fire, and ambulance services.**

## How can we help?

Choose the type of enquiry so your message reaches the right team: Studio Program, Scholarships, Village 21, Mobile Pods, Fundraising enquiries, Media and PR opportunities, or General enquiries.
