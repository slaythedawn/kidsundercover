    yes:
      title: You can apply if you are
      items:
        - Female
        - Aged between 18 and 25 years old
        - Residing in a Kids Under Cover Studio or the main house where the studio is located
        - "Enrolled in a tertiary course in 2025 in a field of Health, Education or Community Services. For example, early childhood, primary or secondary teaching, nursing, mental health, youth work, age support, disability work, dental assistant."
        - Residing in a household where previous scholarship funds have been appropriately acquitted
        - Not a previous recipient of the **Ken & Lenka Morgan, or Sarah Davies Empowering Women Scholarship**
    note: "**If you are unsure if your course is eligible, please email [scholarship@kuc.org.au](mailto:scholarship@kuc.org.au)**"

  - type: faq
    id: faqs
    nav: FAQs
    title: FAQs
    items:
      - question: How many Sarah Davies Scholarships are offered?
        answer: There is one scholarship available per year.
      - question: I have never received a Kids Under Cover Scholarship. Can I apply for the Sarah Davies Empowering Women Scholarship?
        answer: Yes, you can apply for the Sarah Davies Empowering Women Scholarship even if you haven't received a Kids Under Cover Scholarship previously.
      - question: If I apply and am not successful, can I apply in future years?
        answer: Yes, as long as you still meet the eligibility criteria, you can submit an application for the Sarah Davies Empowering Women Scholarship each year.
      - question: I used to live in a Kids Under Cover studio but no longer do, can I still apply for the scholarship?
        answer: Only young people who are currently living in a Kids Under Cover Studio, or young people who are currently living in the main house where the studio is located, are able to apply. However, exceptions may be made if the young person is residing elsewhere temporarily as a direct result of their study / training course (i.e. living in student accommodation).
      - question: I don't live in a Kids Under Cover studio, am I still eligible for a scholarship?
        answer: Yes. If you live in the main house where a Kids Under Cover Studio is located.
      - question: If I am successful in being awarded a Sarah Davies Scholarship, can I still apply for a scholarship next year?
        answer: Yes, you are still eligible for a Kids Under Cover Scholarship the year after receiving the Sarah Davies Empowering Women Scholarship.
