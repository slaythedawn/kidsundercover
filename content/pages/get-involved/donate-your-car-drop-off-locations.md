By dropping off your vehicle, you help reduce transport costs, allowing more of the proceeds from your donation to support young people.

Donate Your Car drop off locations are available in Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Perth, Adelaide, Darwin and Hobart.

## Before you drop off your vehicle

Before dropping off your vehicle, please ensure you have:

- Completed [the Donate Your Car form](https://kuc.my.salesforce-sites.com/Donateyourcar)
- Brought any available keys
- Removed all personal belongings, including e-tags
- Removed your number plates
- Left the owner’s manual and service history (if available)
