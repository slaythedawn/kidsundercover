**Studio Program Evaluation: Seeking Carers’ feedback to ensure we’re meeting the needs of families**

The survey helps to measure the impact of our studios through first-hand accounts of shifts in behaviour, frequency of conflict and the general well-being of young people, among other measures.

## Here are some of the results of the survey

- Frequent risk-taking behaviours such as drug and alcohol use, risky online activity and illegal or dangerous activities decreased from 36% to 5%
- Level of overcrowding in the house decreased from 88% to 24%
- Conflict between family members decreased from 81% down to 16%
- Families who reported getting along well increased from 29% to 96%
- Incidence of time spent away from home without carer knowing whereabouts decreased 51% to 9%
- Young people who demonstrate a low level of independent living skills decreased from 62% to 5%
- Incidence of young person feeling happy increased from 24% to 83%
- Carers’ optimism about the young person’s future increased from 29% to 73%
- Young person almost always meeting school attendance requirements increased from 39% to 64%
- Performance at school, TAFE or university improved from 55% performing well to 89%

## Since receiving a scholarship

- 59% of carers report that the young person has improved attendance rate at school
- 67% report their academic achievement has improved
- 89% of young people plan on finishing their studies (up from 48%)
- 80% of carers report a better ability to purchase necessary school resources

## Testimonials from carers of studio occupants

> “It has given her a safe place for her to be able to recover from a very abusive relationship. It’s given her a space to be able to grow mentally and emotionally, she found a new strength to be able to stand her ground and make good choices. Now that she’s not escaping the house to get away from the overcrowded hectic living conditions.”

> “He is making connections in the community, talking about an apprenticeship, and hoping to be independent once he outgrows the studio. His attitude is completely changed for the better.”

> “They felt like they had hope and that people outside their home do care about their future.”

> “They didn’t seem out of place or embarrassed because we were able to purchase most of the items at the start of the year instead of waiting until I had enough money.”

> “The scholarship allowed him to be a participant in both studies and community involvement as the funding covered the costs of uniforms, books and sporting equipment. Without the funding, the child would not have been able to independently participate due to lack of resources.”

> “Definitely has a better outlook on life, is working and sees a future for themselves. He’s able to make plans for future improvements to life and living.”

> “It has given my son space and independence which helps him focus on his future planning and study.”
