## Join us in ending youth homelessness

The reality is stark. Many of these young people find themselves in severely crowded spaces, couch surfing, or in crisis accommodation that lacks the basic privacy, security, and comfort we often take for granted.

## The Kids Under Cover difference

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/about-montage-3.jpg)

Enter Kids Under Cover, a not-for-profit organisation on a mission to prevent youth homelessness. We don’t just address the problem; we provide tangible solutions. Imagine relocatable, one and two-bedroom studios, complete with bathrooms, built in the backyards of families or carers. These spaces offer the much-needed privacy and security young individuals crave.

But our impact doesn’t stop there. We go further by offering scholarships for education and job training. These scholarships cover basic education costs, empowering young minds to reach their full potential. Importantly, our scholarships extend beyond those residing in our studios, reaching their siblings in the main home.

## A vision of a home for every young person

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/community-fundraise-montage-2.jpg)

Our vision is simple yet profound: No young person in our community should experience homelessness. We believe in creating not just shelter but supportive environments where dreams can flourish.

## Join the movement

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/community-fundraise-montage-3.jpg)

Fundraising is at the heart of what we do. It’s a community effort, and you can be a crucial part of it. Hosting a fundraiser for Kids Under Cover is a powerful way to contribute to our cause. Whether your fundraising concept is big or small, your efforts play a vital role in changing the lives of young people at risk of homelessness.

Ready to make a difference? Start your fundraising journey today, and we’ll be with you every step of the way. Together, we can build a brighter future for the next generation.
