## Business Registration Details and Charitable Status

### Supporting Kids Under Cover

Kids Under Cover is registered as an ‘[Other Incorporated Entity](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/Help/EntityTypeDescription?Id=00017)’ by the [Australian Business Register](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/).

Kids Under Cover’s ABN is: [76 604 592 013](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/76604592013)

**Kids Under Cover is registered with the [Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC)](http://www.acnc.gov.au/) as follows:**

- Kids Under Cover is registered as a charity with ACNC from 03 Dec 2012
- Kids Under Cover is a [Public Benevolent Institution](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/Help/CharitableTaxConcession) endorsed to access the following tax concessions:
  - GST Concession from 01 Jul 2005
  - FBT Exemption from 01 Jul 2005
  - Income Tax Exemption from 01 Jul 2000
- Kids Under Cover is endorsed as a Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) from 01 Jul 2000. It is covered by [Item 1](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/Help/DGR#itaa) of the table in section 30-15 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997

**Further information about Kids Under Cover’s Charitable Status and Business Registration details:**

- ACNC Charity Register Summary
- Notice of endorsement for charity tax concessions
- Endorsement as a Deductible Gift Recipient
- Endorsement as an Income Exempt Charitable Entity
- Certificate of Registration of a Body Corporate as a Company

### Resources and guides for Australians supporting charities through gifts and donations

Kids Under Cover is committed to the safety of all supporters of charities and non-profit organisations. As such, we encourage anyone donating to a charity to consider reading the [Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR)](https://www.abr.business.gov.au/Help/DGR) information available from the Australian Government before making a gift or committing to ongoing regular donations.

**When choosing to support an organisation like Kids Under Cover to do great work in the community, please remember:**

- Making donations is voluntary and always an individual donor’s choice. You might decide on your own to make a donation, or you might be asked, by letter, email, phone or in person, to donate.
- Before you commit to a donation, check to make sure you are contributing via a legitimate donation channel for that charity.
- Check for the charity’s name on the ACNC Charity Register.
- Ask for identification from anyone seeking a donation from you in a face-to-face or telephone setting.
- Be careful of online requests for donations, by checking to make sure that the webpage or email is genuine.
