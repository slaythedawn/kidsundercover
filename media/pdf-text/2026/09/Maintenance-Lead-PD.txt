Maintenance Lead


 Position:                Maintenance Lead

 Department:              Assets and Development

 Time Fraction:           1.0 FTE

 Reports To:              Head of Assets and Development

 Direct Reports:          Maintenance Technicians

 Position Tenure:         Permanent

 Classification:          SCHADS Level 5

 Date of Review:          September 2026

Role Summary

The Maintenance Lead is a newly created role responsible for managing responsive and preventative
maintenance across Kids Under Cover's (KUC) asset portfolio. The role will oversee end-to-end maintenance
processes from triaging requests, scheduling maintenance technicians and contractors through to ensuring
works are completed, documented, and closed out in a timely manner.

The Maintenance Lead will play a key role in lifting the standard and consistency of maintenance operations
at KUC. This includes supporting the onboarding of new contractors through formal procurement processes,
helping to establish and coordinate an electrical and fire safety compliance pipeline.

The role reports to the Head of Assets and Development and works closely with internal teams and external
contractors and service providers to ensure KUC's assets are maintained to a high standard.



Key Responsibility Areas (KRAs)

Responsive Maintenance Management (30%)
       •     Triage incoming maintenance requests and log jobs accurately in the maintenance management
             system

       •     Review and approve quotes from contractors within delegated budget authority, escalating
             higher-value works to the Head of Assets and Development for approval

       •     Coordinate and schedule approved works, managing contractor access and communication with
             relevant internal stakeholders

       •     Monitor works in progress and follow up to ensure timely and quality completion

       •     Close out completed jobs, including reviewing work quality, reconciling costs against approved
             quotes, and retaining relevant documentation

       •     Maintain an accurate and up-to-date log of all maintenance activity across the portfolio

       •   Coordinate emergency and urgent maintenance requests, including acting as the on-call point of
           contact for rare after-hours maintenance issues and ensuring appropriate contractor response is
           mobilised in a timely manner

Management of Maintenance Administration Temp. and Maintenance Technicians (30%)
       •   Oversee the Maintenance Administration Temp’s management of incoming maintenance
           requests, including triage, gathering relevant information, troubleshooting and ensuring requests
           are accurately recorded.
       •   Support the Maintenance Administration Temp. in progressing maintenance requests through
           the quote, approval, work order and contractor engagement process.
       •   Provide day-to-day management of the two Maintenance Technicians and the Maintenance
           Administration Temp, including allocation of work, workload monitoring, priorities, and
           operational support.
       •   Coordinate and oversee the Maintenance Technicians’ schedules, balancing responsive
           maintenance activities with the studio audit program.
       •   Monitor completion of studio audits and ensure maintenance issues identified through audits are
           appropriately recorded, prioritised and progressed.
       •   Support the Maintenance Technicians with complex maintenance matters and provide an
           escalation point for safety, access, household or operational concerns.
       •   Monitor workload and capacity across Maintenance Technician functions, escalating emerging
           risks or resourcing issues to the Head of Assets & Development.

Performance Management and Procurement (15%)
       •   Monitor team performance against agreed service standards, response times, and quality
           expectations. Proactively identify opportunities for improvements in current procedures and
           practices
       •   Coordinate the identification, tendering and onboarding of additional responsive maintenance
           contractors to expand and strengthen KUC's supplier base.
       •   Maintain contractor records including insurances, licences, and compliance documentation,
           ensuring all suppliers meet KUC's requirements

Electrical & Fire Safety Compliance and OHS (15%)
       •   Support the development and coordination of a pipeline for electrical and fire safety checks
           across the asset portfolio
       •   Liaise with relevant contractors to schedule and track compliance inspections and works
       •   Assist in trialling new contractors for compliance works with a view to securing ongoing
           agreements
       •   Maintain records of completed safety checks, certificates, and outstanding actions
       •   Escalate any urgent compliance or OHS issues to the Head of Assets and Development

Administration & Reporting (10%)
       •   Maintain accurate records across all maintenance activity, contractor engagement, and
           compliance obligations
       •   Comply with all relevant KUC policies and procedures, including workplace health and safety
       •   Contribute to reporting on maintenance activity, budgets, and compliance as required
       •   Proactively identify opportunities to improve maintenance processes and practices

Selection Criteria

        •   Demonstrated experience in a maintenance coordination, facilities management, or property
            operations role

        •   Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple work orders and competing
            priorities

        •   Experience liaising with and managing trades contractors, including obtaining and assessing
            quotes

        •   Ability to build and maintain effective working relationships with contractors, suppliers, and
            internal stakeholders

        •   High level of attention to detail, particularly in relation to record keeping, compliance
            documentation, and job tracking

        •   Strong written and verbal communication skills

        •   Ability to work both autonomously and collaboratively within a small team

        •   Proficiency in Microsoft Office, proficiency in Salesforce and the ability to learn new systems
            quickly

        •   Experience with or understanding of procurement/tendering processes (desirable)

        •   Knowledge of electrical and fire safety compliance requirements (desirable)

        •   Current Australian driver's licence and Police Check



Skills & Knowledge

suppliers, and internal teams to deliver quality outcomes.

Critical Thinking – Makes decisions and solves problems through research, reflection, analysis and rigorous

Service Orientation – Delivers a high quality of service to internal and external stakeholders.

Plan and Prioritise – Plan to achieve priority outcomes and respond flexibly to changing circumstances.

Evaluate and Improve – Evaluates processes or programs to drive continuous improvement.

Child Safety Standards - Commitment

We are committed to the safety, participation and empowerment of all children. As part of your role, you may
be working with children and people who are vulnerable. It is your obligation to always ensure their safety and
report any concerns you may have, in line with our Child Safe Policy and Child Safe Code of Conduct.

You will be required to provide a Working with Children check prior to commencement and maintain this check
for the duration of your employment with Kids Under Cover.
