BUILDING STUDIOS,
CHANGING LIVES
Making a life changing difference in the lives of young people and their families
is what drives our work at Kids Under Cover. Preventing young people from
experiencing homelessness is the cornerstone of our work. But the impact of
our programs is more far reaching than this. Thanks to your support, we are
able to keep families together, improve engagement with education and
increase happiness. And this is just the beginning.
Our annual program evaluations explore the impact of Kids Under Cover’s Studio and Scholarship
Programs. Interviews are conducted with carers who have a studio in their backyard. Some of the
