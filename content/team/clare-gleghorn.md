Clare is a leading corporate affairs specialist with two decades of experience working in government, public affairs and corporate communications roles both client and agency-side in Australia and overseas.

She specialises in crisis and issues management, digital reputation, corporate positioning, stakeholder engagement, board, executive and CEO counsel, facilitation and training, communications and business strategy, and creative storytelling.

Clare holds a BA (Journalism)/B. Education (Secondary) and an MBA, and is a member of the Australian Institute of Company Directors.
