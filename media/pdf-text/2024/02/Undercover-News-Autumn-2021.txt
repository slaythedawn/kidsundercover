VILLAGE 21

LAUNCH

The launch of Village 21 in Preston is giving young
people exiting care the chance of a brighter future.

VILLAGE 21'S COMMUNAL OUTDOOR AREA.

A first-of-its-kind, Village 21 provides studios
for six young people, giving them a stable home,
wrap around supports and communal living
elements for up to three years.

Funded by the Victorian Government and
delivered in partnership with Anglicare
Victoria, Village 21 is designed to interrupt
the spiral of homelessness young people
leaving Out of Home Care (OoHC) often find
themselves on.

18-year-old Kiren was one of the first young
people to move into Village 21, having spent most
of his life in OoHC. For young people like Kiren,
the village will provide an opportunity to
successfully transition to independence.

“I've been living at Village 21 for a few months
now. Before this | was sleeping on my mate’s

couch for a month,” Kiren said.

appointed a Member of the Order of

to the commercial retail sector, and to the
community. Elaine shares her experience
of supporting Kids Under Cover and leaving
a gift in her Will to continue her legacy.

| was aware of youth homelessness in Australia,

knowing that | am helping to keep families together
and giving young people the space they need to grow.

A PLACE

TO CALL HOME

You may remember reading Dorothy and Tilly's story with our urgent plea for
support in December. We're so pleased to share that thanks to your support
the family have finally felt the relief of a Kids Under Cover studio and scholarships.

Dorothy took in her three grandchildren, Tilly, Jack
and Ben, when their mother was no longer able to
care for them. The overcrowding in Dorothy's small
home was pushing the family to breaking point, and
it was getting harder every year as the kids grew.

When Tilly and her brothers saw the studio in their
Grandma Dorothy's yard, they were very excited.

“| thought it was cool and nice, because I'd be able to
put my belongings in and not have them scattered all
around... | was able to make it the space that | wanted
it to be,” said Tilly.

For her brother Jack, the studio made a big difference

in his ability to study for school, and to connect with
his friends. “Having the space has helped a lot, | can
concentrate and focus more on my studies now
which is good. Plus, | can have mates over to

watch the footy!”

Thinking about the impact of the studio, Dorothy
reflects, "the kids used to get quite cross at me and
my husband, because things were hard, and we were
the ones there, you know, they didn't have their mum
and dad to take out their frustrations with, so | used
to just say to them ‘Nan and pop are doing the best

DOROTHY AND TILLY.

Now Dorothy says, “the kids are much happier
within themselves, much more content.”

And it's even given Dorothy's family back some
quality time and space. Dorothy says, “It's given

us all space to spread out. | don't have to be making

every time someone comes to visit, | don’t have say,
‘I'm sorry about the mattress on the floor, but the
grandkids sleep there.’”

“The studio has been an absolute godsend”
says Dorothy.
