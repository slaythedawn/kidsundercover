Christopher joined the Board in December 2021, after becoming involved with Kids Under Cover a decade earlier through pro-bono consulting.

He strongly believes in the combined preventative approach of education and housing to support young people.

Christopher is currently the Head of Member Strategy for a major superannuation fund, and brings his professional skills to Kids Under Cover offering strategic frameworks to support long-term planning and robust oversight of organisational outcomes.
