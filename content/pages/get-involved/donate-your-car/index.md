  - type: faq
    id: faqs
    nav: FAQs
    tone: card
    title: Donate Your Car FAQs
    intro: Got questions? We've got answers.
    more: { label: Call 1800 801 633, href: "tel:1800801633" }
    items:
      - question: What kinds of vehicles will you accept?
        answer: |
          We accept cars, vans, motorbikes, caravans, trucks, buses, tractors, boats on trailers, and much more. Have a different vehicle? Contact us to find out if we can accept your donation.

          We are able to accept vehicles that have general wear and tear, however we're unable to accept anything with significant damage, such as smashed windows, major panel damage, mould or missing parts.
      - question: Do you offer car removal?
        answer: Yes. Donating is an alternative to paying for car removal or selling for scrap. We arrange collection in metropolitan Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Adelaide, Perth, Darwin and Hobart. Read more about [car removal by donation](/car-removal/) or [car removal in Melbourne](/car-removal/melbourne/).
      - question: Can I donate a car that isn't running?
        answer: Yes. We're able to accept cars that aren't running, as long as the battery and engine are both intact and nothing has been removed from the vehicle.
      - question: Where will you collect vehicles from?
        answer: |
          We collect vehicles within metropolitan Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Adelaide, Perth, Darwin, and Hobart.

          We are able to collect cars from regional areas, however as these towing costs are higher we will ask if you are able to contribute towards the cost of towing your car. If the car is driveable, there is also the option of dropping the car off at the closest Manheim auction site.
      - question: How much money will my car sell for at auction?
        answer: All our donated vehicles are sold by Manheim's experienced auctioneers at their public auctions. Regardless of what you believe your car might be worth, the car is worth exactly what someone is prepared to pay for it at auction. This might be more than, equal to or less than you expect.
      - question: Will I receive a tax receipt for my car donation?
        answer: |
          Yes, all vehicles donated to Kids Under Cover are tax deductible. You can expect to receive your tax receipt for the sale amount of the vehicle at auction within 8 to 12 weeks of submitting your donation. This will be sent via Australia Post unless otherwise specified.

          The date on your receipt will be the date Kids Under Cover received the sale proceeds from your vehicle. The receipt must be issued in the name of the last or current registered owner of the vehicle.
      - question: What happens to the money from my car?
        answer: Once sold, the proceeds go directly to fund Kids Under Cover programs. The sale of your car can help deliver and expand both our studio and scholarship programs, helping a young person stay connected to education, family and community.
