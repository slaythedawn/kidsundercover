**Safe housing key to reducing youth justice involvement, new report finds.**

The report, *Giving them a chance*, highlights the impact of the Kids Under Cover Studio Program, showing that early intervention and safe housing, paired with the right support services, can help break cycles of homelessness, disadvantage and justice system involvement while delivering better outcomes for young people and the broader community.
