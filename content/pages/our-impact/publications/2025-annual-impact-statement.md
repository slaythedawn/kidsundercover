    title: Mobile Pods
    body: |
      The Mobile Pod pilot continued to expand over the year through the generous support of the Paul Ramsay Foundation. The program provides a more rapid accommodation solution for young people at imminent risk of homelessness, with a focus on those who have experienced out-of-home care or engagement with the youth justice system.

      A new type of backyard pod has been designed to be constructed on site, in backyards where a pod on wheels could not access. A real time evaluation of the pilot is underway, with early data demonstrating the benefits for young people and families.

  - type: text
    title: A mobile pod case study
    body: |
      The provision of a mobile pod created an opportunity to safely trial family reunification for a 15-year-old male following time in out-of-home care. This reunification would otherwise not have been possible.

      Since living in the mobile pod, the carer has observed noticeable improvements in the young person's behaviour and emotional wellbeing, and was pleased to report that he was not engaged in any offending. He is looking for work and is enjoying an improved relationship with his mum and her partner.

      The mobile pod was in place and used by the young person for almost six months. Following its success in enabling family reunification, Child Protection were able to source a long-term housing solution for the young person. This mobile pod can now be re-deployed to help the next young person in need.
