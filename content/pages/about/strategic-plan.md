  - type: features
    id: priorities
    nav: Priorities
    columns: 2
    eyebrow: Strategic priorities 2026
    title: Four strategic priorities
    items:
      - icon: studio
        title: 1. Innovate for impact
        text: "**Focus:** deliver and scale innovative housing solutions and programs that create lasting change for young people at risk of or experiencing homelessness. **Commitments:** leverage relocatable accommodation to unlock new pathways of support; strengthen how we measure evidence and communicate outcomes and impact; expand our role in connecting young people to education, training and employment; scale and sustain our diverse portfolio of proven accommodation solutions; mobilise strategic partnerships to drive impact; co-design diverse housing solutions with communities, including better supporting First Nations and LGBTQIA+ young people and other vulnerable cohorts."
      - icon: people
        title: 2. Co-design and youth voice
        text: "**Focus:** ensure young people actively shape the Kids Under Cover programs, services and decisions that affect them. **Commitments:** create clear and accessible pathways for young people to participate in decision-making; co-design programs and solutions with young people, alongside community and sector partners; embed continuous feedback loops to listen, learn and adapt; scale approaches proven to work, guided by lived experience and evidence; use data and insight to respond to identified needs."
      - icon: book
        title: 3. Thought leadership and advocacy
        text: "**Focus:** lead and influence the sector through prevention-focused, early-intervention and evidence-informed advocacy. **Commitments:** champion systems change that embeds prevention and early intervention as central to ending youth homelessness; position Kids Under Cover as a trusted leader in innovative, prevention-focused solutions; generate, curate and share evidence to inform policy, practice and innovation; demonstrate the economic and social value of our work, including avoided costs and social return on investment."
      - icon: leaf
        title: 4. Focused sustainable growth
        text: "**Focus:** grow with purpose to maximise impact while strengthening long-term financial and organisational sustainability. **Commitments:** align growth with sustainable funding models, delivery capacity and workforce capability; ensure all growth is risk-assessed, financially viable and impact-driven; build organisational adaptability to respond to changing need, policy and funding environments; pursue selective expansion where demand, partnerships and capability align with our model and expertise."

  - type: text
    id: welcome
    nav: Welcome
    eyebrow: Welcome
    title: A message from our Chair and CEO
    body: |
      Every night, Kids Under Cover provides safe, supported accommodation to more than 1,000 young people at risk of leaving home prematurely and entering cycles of homelessness, out-of-home care and youth justice. Yet the need far outweighs our current reach. Today, we can meet only around 5% of demand.

      It is confronting to read their stories, and deeply frustrating to see limited government investment in a proven model that prevents homelessness before it takes hold and reduces demand in high-cost systems like out-of-home care.

      There are many pathways into youth homelessness that we will seek to disrupt, helping individuals and changing systems. We will innovate for impact, centre youth voice through co-design, provide strong thought leadership and advocacy, and pursue focused, sustainable growth.

      The evidence is clear and the solutions are proven. We invite governments, partners and supporters to act with us to stop youth homelessness before it starts.

      **Clare Gleghorn**, Chair, and **Stephen Nash**, Chief Executive Officer, Kids Under Cover
