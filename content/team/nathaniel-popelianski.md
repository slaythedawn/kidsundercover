Nathaniel is a senior partner at Corrs Chambers Westgarth in the Real Estate Practice. He acts for global and national occupiers and investors, government and housing associations on large scale real estate transactions and has a strong focus on mixed-use, commercial office and social and affordable housing developments.

He is recognised as a leading Australian real estate and leasing lawyer in numerous legal directories including Chambers Asia Pacific, Legal 500 and Best Lawyers Peer Review.

As the Chair of our Board, Nathaniel helps ensure that Kids Under Cover governance practices are within the law and our constitution. He is passionate about preventing youth homelessness and making a lasting impact on the lives of young people and their communities through early intervention and support.

Nathaniel joined the Kids Under Cover board in August 2012, was elected Vice Chair in November 2017 and Chair in June 2022, and has recently transitioned to continue his contribution as a Director.
