Jasmin has recently joined Kids Under Cover as Head of Fundraising and Communications.

Jasmin is an experienced leader with a strong background in marketing, fundraising and engagement across the not-for-profit sector. Her career spans across organisations in the sector, including SANE, Whitelion and McGrath Foundation.

Jasmin is dedicated to expanding the long-term impact of Kids Under Cover on young people and their families through strategic fundraising and effective communications initiatives.
