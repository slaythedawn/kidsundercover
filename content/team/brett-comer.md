Brett has held executive positions in the health and human services, financial services, and early childcare education sectors. His primary focus is collaborating with others to achieve impactful and sustainable outcomes for customers, the community, and related stakeholders.

With a Bachelor of Business in Accounting, he is also a Fellow of Chartered Accountants Australia and New Zealand and a graduate of the Australian Institute of Company Directors.

Brett provides interim executive support to CFOs, CEOs, Boards and their teams during leadership transitions, complex transformations and strategic transactions. His experience includes leading strategic transformation and enterprise-wide initiatives in finance, customer experience, startups and turnarounds in private, publicly owned and purpose organisations including Bupa, Avant Mutual, HealthShare Victoria, State Trustees, Monash IVF and Camp Australia.

Brett is passionate about positive change and committed to using his extensive experience to contribute to Kids Under Cover’s mission to combat youth homelessness by creating studios with family members or carers and providing education and training scholarships.
