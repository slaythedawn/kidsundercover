By reactivating your monthly support, you’ll continue providing young people at risk of homelessness with the stability and hope they need. Your commitment helps us work toward a future where no young person is homeless.

To resume your regular donation, simply update your payment details below. Every donation over $2 remains tax-deductible, and we’re here to assist if you have any questions.

Thank you for standing with us. Together, we can make a lasting difference.

You can read more about our charitable status [here](https://www.kuc.org.au/about/contact/kids-under-cover-charitable-status-and-tax-information/).

## Donation details

- **$19** could help a young person settle into their new KUC studio
- **$39** could help a young person stay connected to their education, peers and community via our scholarship program
- **$59** could help provide a young person with a stable and secure place to call home and reduce their risk of homelessness

## Support us with a one-off donation

Every donation, no matter the size or frequency does make a difference to young people at risk of homelessness. We appreciate every contribution, and thank you for donating when you’re able to.

## Become a regular giver

We know not everyone can commit to a recurring donation, but for those who can afford to do so, you’ll be helping us to make longer term plans for the programs we deliver for young people with the peace of mind that we can count on your commitment to donate on a regular basis.

This can be via a direct debit or credit card payment set up to automatically deduct on the frequency of your choice. Call us on 1800 801 633 to discuss your regular giving plan.

## Workplace giving

A regular, pre-tax donation straight from your pay is easy to set up and you receive tax benefits. Workplace giving even provides your employer the opportunity to match your donation dollar for dollar.

Ask us for more information by emailing us or calling **1800 801 633**.

[Get in touch by emailing us](mailto:fundraise@kuc.org.au)

## Donate Your Car®

Make a significant contribution to the lives of young Australians who are homeless or at risk of becoming homeless. It’s free, tax deductible, and a fast alternative to selling or trading your unwanted vehicle.

[Donate a car to Kids Under Cover](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/donate-your-car/)

## Leave a gift in your Will

Your generous decision to leave a bequest to Kids Under Cover is a promise to support young people and help them thrive well into the future. With your gift, you could have an incredible impact on the lives of young people by safeguarding our work for years to come.

[Learn more about bequesting](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/bequests/)
