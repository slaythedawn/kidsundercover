                   Acknowledgement
                   We acknowledge our privilege to live and work on Aboriginal land and
                   acknowledge the enduring strength and resilience of all Aboriginal and Torres
                   Strait Islander peoples and culture.

                   We recognise the deep continuing connections that First Nations Peoples have
                   to land, waterways, wildlife and country.

                   Our teams are committed to helping amplify the voices of the young First
                   Nations people in the communities we work with. And to doing everything we
                   can to erase the inequities that exist as they strive to reach their full potential.

                   We look forward to the opportunities for learning, growth, connection, and
                   transformation that this journey will bring for our organisation. Our gratitude
                   and acknowledgement extends to the many First Nations partners and friends
                   who share their expertise, wisdom and perspectives with us so generously.

Our Village 21 model is designed to
accommodate and support young people
who are exiting care and who do not have
the supports in place to live independently.
We co-locate multiple studios on one site
where young people can have their own safe
space to call home, while also experiencing
elements of communal living with other
residents: including shared kitchen, living,
dining and laundry. The Village 21 model
also includes two live-in mentors who live
on site, while a key worker attends regularly
to provide guidance on employment
opportunities, educational pathways, and
independent living skills.

Three new Village 21 projects are currently
in development (including two in regional
Victoria) all with a focus on accommodating
young people from Aboriginal and Torres
Strait Islander communities. For these
projects, we are partnering with Aboriginal
Community Controlled Organisations
including Victorian Aboriginal Child
Care Agency (VACCA), Rumbalara and
Gippsland & East Gippsland Aboriginal
Co-Operative (GEGAC) to lead the service
delivery component and ensure that First
Nations perspectives, insights and cultural
considerations are embedded throughout
the village design.

Kids Under Cover employs 24 people in our
office in Richmond, Victoria to deliver our
services throughout Victoria and South
Australia. None of these people identify as
First Nations peoples. Our models currently
extend across metropolitan and regional
Victoria and South Australia.
