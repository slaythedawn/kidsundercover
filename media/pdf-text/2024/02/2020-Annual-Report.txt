          Lucy’s Story
          13-year old Lucy lives with her
          grandmother Marj and an eight-
          year-old foster child. Life in their
          small two-bedroom home was
          hard, sharing a bedroom meant
          neither girl had privacy or space
          to be themselves. Marj knew this
          overcrowding increased the risk of
          Lucy leaving home and experiencing
          homelessness.
          Everything changed when the family
          received a Kids Under Cover studio
          for Lucy in November 2019.
          Lucy now has a stable and secure
          place to call home, and the space
          she needs to thrive.

ABOUT US
Kids Under Cover is leading the way in youth
homelessness prevention in Australia.

Our early intervention and prevention model interrupts
the spiral of homelessness for young people aged 12-25.




                                                                   1,789
By combining stable and secure studio accommodation with
education scholarships, we prevent homelessness and help
young people thrive. Our programs are life-changing for
vulnerable young people and their families.

We have achieved a lot over the past 30 years, but while
there is still youth homelessness in our community there
is much to be done. When it comes to youth homelessness,

     Riley’s Story — RELIEF DURING COVID-19
     For vulnerable young people, the COVID-19 pandemic and stay at
     home restrictions exacerbated the risk of homelessness.

     With two adults and seven children aged 9-20 in a four-bedroom
     home, there was no space for anyone in this family to relax, study or
     socialise. The overcrowding pushed eldest son Riley and his partner
     out into a private rental. However expensive living costs and job
     insecurity forced them to move back into the overcrowded home. This
     quickly took its toll on the whole family. Then COVID-19 began and
     having to stay home only amplified the impact of the overcrowding.

     “It was just so stressful. Everyone was getting frustrated and there
     was so much tension,” explains mum Lisette.

     A Kids Under Cover studio couldn’t have come at a better time.
     In June 2020 a one-bedroom studio was built in the family’s
     backyard for Riley and his partner Natasha.

     “We are so rapt, it’s a godsend. There’s so much more room in
     the house and Riley and Natasha are loving having their own space
     in the studio. The kids are so much happier,” Lisette tells us.

     Jesse’s Story — KEN & LENKA
     MORGAN SCHOLARSHIP
     Before receiving his Kids Under Cover studio in 2017,
     Jesse was at risk of homelessness. Jesse was in the
     foster care system and his home was overcrowded
     with seven other people living there. After moving
     into his studio, Jesse received a Kids Under Cover
     scholarship to support him and continue his education.
     This year, Jesse was the recipient of the Ken & Lenka
     Morgan Scholarship.

     Jesse used the scholarship to travel overseas and take
     part in an intensive medical program in a leading Czech
     hospital, bringing him one step closer to his dream of
     becoming a surgeon. Jesse is currently completing his
     Bachelor of Biomedicine and interviewing for medical
     schools. We can’t wait to see what his bright future holds.

Donate Your Car is one of Kids Under Cover’s
most successful fundraising initiatives. It
provides a hassle-free way to find a new
purpose for an unneeded vehicle, while making
a valuable donation to help prevent youth
homelessness. We organise the collection of
the vehicle and it is then sold by our auction
partner Manheim. Since its inception in 2006,
Donate Your Car has received more than 20,000
donations. All profits from the sale support
our Studio and Scholarship Programs. It’s not
just cars, we also take boats, vans, trailers,
motorbikes, caravans, trucks, tractors and even
buses! In the 2020 financial year 2,009 vehicles
were donated.
