        title: How long can occupants stay?
        text: Young people can live at Village 21 Bairnsdale until the end of their 21st year. Throughout their stay, the Key Practitioner works alongside each young person to help them prepare for independent living and secure long-term housing prior to their exit.
      - icon: people
        title: What is the role of the mentors?
        text: Live-in Mentors are volunteers who live at the property. At least one mentor is expected to be present at Village 21 Bairnsdale each night. They are available to assist with building the independent living skills of young people, and model independent living behaviours such as attending work or study, cleaning, cooking, washing, etc.
