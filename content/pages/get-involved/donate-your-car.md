## Make a life-changing difference for a young person in need

By donating your unwanted vehicle, you’ll help us raise vital funds to deliver life-changing accommodation and education programs that give vulnerable young people at risk of homelessness the brighter futures they deserve.

## Your car will help stop homelessness before it starts

The Donate Your Car® program has helped raise over $23 million to date, providing funding for programs that help vulnerable young people aged 12-25. The sale of your car can help deliver and expand both our studio and scholarship programs, helping a young person stay connected to education, family and community.

[Learn more about our programs](https://www.kuc.org.au/our-programs/)

[Watch the video: Your car. Their new beginning.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Vmt1-5dVN4)

## Five easy steps

**Step one:** [Complete the form](https://kuc.my.salesforce-sites.com/Donateyourcar) to tell us about the car you want to donate.

**Step two:** Our team will get in touch to arrange transporting your vehicle to the auction house.

**Step three:** Your car will be sold at auction.

**Step four:** Once sold, the proceeds will go directly to fund the Kids Under Cover programs, and we’ll send you a tax receipt so you can claim a deduction.

**Step five:** We’ll deliver studio accommodation and other supports to young people and help them to change the course of their lives.

## Help your donation go even further

When you donate your vehicle to Kids Under Cover, you’re already making a meaningful difference in the lives of young people at risk of homelessness. Here’s how you can maximise the impact of your donation.

### Drop off your vehicle

If your vehicle is safe and driveable, you can drop it off at a [designated auction site](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/donate-your-car-drop-off-locations/), we’ll thank you with a $100 Smart Prezzee eGift Card, redeemable at the following retailers: **Uber, Didi, Coles, Woolworths, Ampol, Shell and JB Hi-Fi.**

By dropping off your vehicle, you help reduce transport costs, allowing more of the proceeds from your donation to support young people.

[Learn more about drop-off locations](https://www.kuc.org.au/get-involved/donate-your-car-drop-off-locations/)

Terms and conditions apply. [See the Smart Prezzee eGift Card terms and conditions](https://www.kuc.org.au/smart-prezzee-egift-card-terms-and-conditions/).

### Contribute to towing

Can’t drop off your vehicle? We can arrange collection.

If you’d like, you can make an optional contribution towards towing costs. This helps direct more of your donation to our Studio and Scholarship programs, supporting young people at risk of homelessness.

Thank you for your vehicle donation whether you choose to contribute to towing, or not.

*NB: Vehicles located in regional areas may incur additional collection and transport costs.*

## Donate Your Car FAQs

Got questions? We’ve got answers. Here’s some more information to help you with your decision to donate your car to charity.

### What kinds of vehicles will you accept?

We accept cars, vans, motorbikes, caravans, trucks, buses, tractors, boats on trailers, and much more. Have a different vehicle? Contact us to find out if we can accept your donation.

We are able to accept vehicles that have general wear and tear, however we’re unable to accept anything with significant damage, i.e. smashed windows, major panel damage, mould, missing parts etc.

We’re able to accept cars that aren’t running, as long as the battery/engine are both intact and nothing has been removed from the vehicle.

### Where will you collect vehicles from?

We collect vehicles within metropolitan Melbourne, Sydney, Newcastle, Brisbane, Townsville, Adelaide, Perth, Darwin, and Hobart.

We are able to collect cars from regional areas, however as these towing costs are higher we will ask if you are able to contribute towards the cost of towing your car.

If the car is driveable, there is also the option of dropping the car off at the closest Manheim auction site.

### How much money will my car sell for at auction?

All our donated vehicles are sold by Manheim’s experienced auctioneers at their public auctions. Please be aware that regardless of what you believe your car might be worth, the car is worth exactly what someone is prepared to pay for it at auction. This might be more than, equal to or less than you expect.

### Will I receive a tax receipt for my car donation?

Yes, all vehicles donated to Kids Under Cover are tax deductible! You can expect to receive your tax receipt for the sale amount of the vehicle at auction within 8-12 weeks of submitting your donation so you can claim a tax deduction. This will be sent via Australia Post unless otherwise specified.

The date on your receipt will be the date Kids Under Cover received the sale proceeds from your vehicle.

The tax-deductible donation receipt must be issued in the name of the last/current registered owner of the vehicle.
