STRATEGIC PRIORITY 1
INNOVATE FOR IMPACT
Focus: Deliver and scale innovative housing solutions and
programs that create lasting change for young people at
risk of or experiencing homelessness.

Commitments:
  Leverage relocatable accommodation to unlock new
  pathways of support for vulnerable young people.

  Strengthen how we measure evidence and communicate
  outcomes and impact.

  Expand our role in connecting young people to education,
  training and employment.

  Scale and sustain our diverse portfolio of proven
  accommodation solutions.

  Mobilise strategic partnerships to drive impact.

  Co-design diverse housing solutions with communities,
  including better supporting First Nations and LGBTQIA+
  young people and other vulnerable cohorts.
STRATEGIC PRIORITY 2
CO-DESIGN
& YOUTH VOICE
Focus: Ensure young people actively shape the Kids Under
Cover programs, services and decisions that affect them.

Commitments:
  Create clear and accessible pathways for young people
  to participate in decision-making.

  Co-design programs and solutions with young people,
  alongside community and sector partners.

  Embed continuous feedback loops to listen, learn and
  adapt.

  Scale approaches proven to work, guided by lived
  experience and evidence.

  Use data and insight to respond to identified needs.
STRATEGIC PRIORITY 3
THOUGHT LEADERSHIP
& ADVOCACY
Focus: Lead and influence the sector through prevention-
focused, early-intervention and evidence-informed advocacy.

Commitments:
  Champion systems change that embeds prevention
  and early intervention as central to ending youth
  homelessness.

  Position Kids Under Cover as a trusted leader in innovative,
  prevention-focused solutions.

  Generate, curate and share evidence to inform policy,
  practice and innovation.

  Demonstrate the economic and social value of our work,
  including avoided costs and social return on investment.
STRATEGIC PRIORITY 4
FOCUSED
SUSTAINABLE GROWTH
Focus: Grow with purpose to maximise impact while
strengthening long-term financial and organisational
sustainability.

Commitments:
  Align growth with sustainable funding models, delivery
  capacity and workforce capability.

  Ensure all growth is risk-assessed, financially viable and
  impact-driven.

  Build organisational adaptability to respond to changing
  need, policy and funding environments.

  Pursue selective expansion where demand, partnerships
  and capability align with our model and expertise.

ABOUT US
Kids Under Cover was founded in 1989 by businessman and philanthropist, Ken Morgan AO
in response to the public awareness raised by the Burdekin Inquiry into Youth Homelessness.
Ken was driven by the simple notion that by creating stability and opportunity we could enable
young people to achieve their hopes and dreams.

Now, more than 35 years later, we remain committed to building brighter futures for young
people through our programs and partnerships, driven by the core belief that no young person
in our community should be homeless.

We are a registered charitable organisation in Australia supporting vulnerable young
people between the ages of 12 and 25 years who are either already homeless or are at risk of
homelessness. We currently house over 1000 young people each night.
