## “The Kids Under Cover Studio was life altering, instantly. It was like Christmas x100!”

Rachel’s family have a Kids Under Cover studio in their backyard. This enables them to welcome and care for foster children with nowhere else to go. Foster families like Rachel’s are among those we help with customised housing solutions.

## What do you do when you have a heart full of love, but a house that’s bursting at the seams?

**Donate today.** Show kids you care. We'll email your tax receipt and donation details.

- **$50:** Here’s my gift to help set up a studio, so a young person has a place to rest, study and belong.
- **$125:** Here’s my gift to help maintain the studio program, ensuring young people continue to feel supported and connected.
- **$250:** Here’s my gift to help expand the studio program, giving young people a safe and stable space to thrive.

Rachel shares your deep compassion for children in need. As a foster carer, she’s welcomed more than 100 vulnerable kids into her home. These kids have lived through unimaginable trauma, and they have left dysfunctional living situations. So when Rachel’s biological children began to grow, and space became scarce, it broke her heart to begin turning children away.

> “It’s tough when you don’t have room for kids who need it… **It still makes me want to cry when I think of it…** I was bursting at the seams already.”

> “All my kids were squashed into a couple of rooms and on top of each other while my biological boys were trying to study… and I had a newborn screaming the house down. It was a nightmare.”

The two-bedroom Kids Under Cover studio changed everything for Rachel’s family. When they first moved into the studio, Rachel's biological children Jacob and Finn had the privacy and quiet they needed.

Meanwhile, it freed up bedroom space in the main house so Rachel could foster more vulnerable children.

Today your Christmas gift helps Kids Under Cover continue to support vulnerable young people at risk of or experiencing homelessness by giving them a safe space to thrive.

> “I can't foster without the studio because my biological children need a room. Without it, we wouldn't have the space to welcome others… and I wouldn’t have been able to help anywhere near the number of I have.”

Lucas is now 16. He’s thriving and a dearly loved member of the family. And you can give this incredible gift to another vulnerable child today with your Christmas gift.

Today your compassion means so much more than another room or extra space. It’s the beginning of belonging. You’re giving a child the difference between having nowhere to go and a home where they finally belong.
