    title: Our acknowledgement
    body: |
      We acknowledge our privilege to live and work on Aboriginal land and acknowledge the enduring strength and resilience of all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples and culture.

      We recognise the deep continuing connections that First Nations Peoples have to land, waterways, wildlife and country.

      Our teams are committed to helping amplify the voices of the young First Nations people in the communities we work with. And to doing everything we can to erase the inequities that exist as they strive to reach their full potential.

      We look forward to the opportunities for learning, growth, connection, and transformation that this journey will bring for our organisation. Our gratitude and acknowledgement extends to the many First Nations partners and friends who share their expertise, wisdom and perspectives with us so generously.

    eyebrow: Our RAP
    text: We want to **deepen our organisational commitment to reconciliation**, strengthen our relationships and partnerships with First Nations communities, and amplify the voices and experiences of young people from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander backgrounds.
    body: At Kids Under Cover, we strive to deepen our understanding of the connection to country and culture. We are actively involved in strengthening our partnerships with First Nations-led organisations, as their expertise and guidance are essential in ensuring that our supported accommodation model is developed with cultural safety in mind, and that it is informed by the lived experiences and insights of First Nations peoples.

    body: |
      In collaboration with GEGAC, Rumbalara and VACCA, we are developing three villages specifically designed for Aboriginal young people transitioning out of care between the ages of 18 to 21. These villages will be named using local Aboriginal languages.

      The first project named is **Moorumbina Mongurnallin**, the Village 21 project in Frankston in partnership with VACCA, which was named with input from young Aboriginal people and means 'you are loved' in local Bunurong language.

  - type: features
    id: commitments
    nav: Commitments
    columns: 2
    tone: card
    eyebrow: Our commitments
    title: Relationships, respect, opportunities and governance
    items:
      - icon: people
        title: Relationships
        text: Establish and strengthen mutually beneficial relationships with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander stakeholders and organisations. Build relationships through celebrating National Reconciliation Week. Promote reconciliation through our sphere of influence. Promote positive race relations through anti-discrimination strategies, including a review of studio and scholarship applications and policies.
      - icon: leaf
        title: Respect
        text: Increase understanding, value and recognition of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultures, histories, knowledge and rights through cultural learning. Demonstrate respect by observing cultural protocols, including Acknowledgement of Country and Welcome to Country. Build respect by celebrating NAIDOC Week.
      - icon: key
        title: Opportunities
        text: Improve employment outcomes by increasing Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander recruitment, retention and professional development. Increase Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander supplier diversity to support improved economic and social outcomes, including investigating Supply Nation membership.
      - icon: shield
        title: Governance
        text: Establish and maintain an effective RAP Working Group, with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander representation, to drive governance of the RAP. Provide appropriate support for effective implementation. Build accountability and transparency through reporting to Reconciliation Australia. Continue our reconciliation journey by developing our next RAP.
