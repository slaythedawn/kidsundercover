Imagine relocatable, one and two-bedroom studios, complete with bathrooms, built in the backyards of families or carers. These scholarships cover basic education costs, empowering young minds to reach their full potential. Importantly, our scholarships extend beyond those residing in our studios, reaching their siblings in the main home.
