    nav: Village 21
    title: Launch of Moorumbina Mongurnallin
    body: |
      This year marked the launch of our 2nd Village 21, Moorumbina Mongurnallin, a Bunurong phrase meaning "You are loved". This name was decided in consultation with young people.

      In partnership with VACCA (Victorian Aboriginal Child and Community Agency), we have created a model that provides stability and wraparound support services for young Aboriginal care leavers.

      Young people have started settling into newly built homes that offer both stability and a community-focused model of support, including education, employment, and mental health services.

  - type: split
    id: mobile-pods
    nav: Mobile Pods
    flip: true
    title: Mobile Pods
    body: |
      Kids Under Cover was pleased to launch our Mobile Pod Pilot this year, a new initiative to prevent and resolve youth homelessness. It is testing a new and rapid accommodation solution for those on the brink of family breakdown and homelessness or needing reunification or a rapid placement from out-of-home-care or youth justice.

      Thanks to funding from Trusts and Foundations, seven Mobile Pods have been constructed and are being rolled out to young people in need. An evaluation and measurement framework and steering committee has been established.

    body: |
      In November we hosted our Partnering for Impact event, generously supported by Corrs Chambers Westgarth. We discussed the growing issue of youth homelessness in Victoria, where over 7,600 young people are affected each night, and the urgent need for funding in prevention and early intervention.

      We were honoured to hear from The Hon. Harriet Shing MP, who spoke about the importance of systemic change in addressing homelessness. Corey Harrison from VACCA also discussed the value of our collaboration to provide culturally supportive spaces for young people.
