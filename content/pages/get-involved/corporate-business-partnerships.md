## Make a difference with Kids Under Cover

From corporate volunteering to workplace giving, or even brand partnerships, we are grateful for the unwavering support from our generous partners, and we invite businesses like yours to join us in making a lasting impact on the lives of young people.

## Why partner with us?

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/corporate-montage-2.jpg)

Align your business with a cause that matters and demonstrate your commitment to local communities. By partnering with Kids Under Cover, you not only set yourself apart from competitors but also enhance employee well-being, job satisfaction, and corporate citizenship.

Together, we contribute to breaking the cycle of youth homelessness while achieving your Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) goals.

## Partnering with Kids Under Cover

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/corporate-montage-3.jpg)

Tailored to meet the objectives of business, philanthropic, and individual partners, a Kids Under Cover partnership offers various ways to support our vision and mission.

## Many ways to show your support

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/corporate-montage-4.jpg)

Join hands with us to strengthen bonds while contributing to a better future.

Your team can participate in enabling many aspects of Kids Under Cover’s mission to end youth homelessness, from contributing vital funds through workplace giving initiatives, to corporate volunteering or in-kind business service sponsorship; or even simply helping us spread the word about youth homelessness and how we aim to solve this issue for young Australians.

## Ready to make a difference together?

![](https://www.kuc.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/scholarships-montage.jpg)

Email us at [kuc@kuc.org.au](mailto:kuc@kuc.org.au) or call (03) 9429 7444 to explore partnership opportunities. Your commitment can change lives and build a brighter future.
